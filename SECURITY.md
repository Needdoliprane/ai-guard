# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

A private vulnerability-reporting channel is still to be established. Keep vulnerabilities and reproduction details out of repository issues, pull requests, and discussions. The maintainer will document how reports are received, triaged, and answered after the private channel has been tested.

Prepare the following information for a private report.

*   A clear and concise description of the issue.
*   Steps to reproduce the issue.
*   An assessment of the potential impact.
